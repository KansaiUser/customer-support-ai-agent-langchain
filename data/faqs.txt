2. Financial, Refund, & Cancellation Policies
This section contains strict legal and financial constraints. The AI must compute eligibility before escalating to billing.

The 7-Day Conditional Refund Policy
Students can request a refund within 7 calendar days of their cohort start date (Day 1 is the official cohort launch day, not the payment day). However, a refund is strictly voided if either of the following conditions is met:

The user has consumed more than 15% of the total course video modules.

The user has downloaded any offline source code assets or specialized course e-books.

The Refund Math Formula: All approved refunds are subject to a non-refundable $150 administrative and registration fee.

Example Calculation: If a Full-Stack student ($2,400) cancels on Day 4 and has watched 5% of the videos, their approved refund amount is exactly $2,250 ($2,400 - $150).

Installments & EMI Defaults
Grace Period: Monthly installments are due on the 1st of each month. A 5-day grace period is automatically applied.

Account Suspension: On the 6th of the month, if payment fails, the system automatically revokes access to the live mentor links and the coding playground.

Late Fee: A flat fee of $25 is automatically appended to the invoice on the 7th of the month. Access cannot be restored by support staff manually; the student must clear the balance via their dashboard.


3. Academic Operations & Batch Management
Batch Transfer Requests
Students frequently ask to move to a future cohort due to work or personal emergencies.

Within first 14 days: Free, automated transfer allowed, provided the target cohort has open seats (cap is 50 students per cohort).

Day 15 to Day 45: Permitted only under medical/professional emergencies with documentation. A $100 batch-migration fee applies.

After Day 45: Strictly prohibited. The student must instead withdraw and re-enroll with a 30% returning-student discount.

Attendance & Certification
To receive an Industry-Certified Sync-Credential, a student must maintain a minimum 75% live class attendance record AND score at least 70% on the Capstone Project evaluation.

If a live class is missed, watching the recorded session on the dashboard within 48 hours counts as "delayed attendance" for up to 5 classes maximum per track.

4. Technical Support Runbooks
If a student reports a technical glitch, walk them through these technical protocols before escalating a ticket to Engineering.

Issue A: Coding Playground Fails to Initialize
The built-in browser IDE relies on WebGL and secure WebSockets (wss://).

Check Browser Compatibility: Confirm they are using Chrome (v110+), Edge (v110+), or Safari (v16+). Firefox requires changing network.websocket.allowInsecureFromHTTPS to true in advanced settings if they are behind certain corporate proxies.

Ad-Blocker Interference: Extensions like uBlock Origin, Brave Shield, or Privacy Badger frequently block the isolated iframe container (sandbox.skillsync.ai). The student must whitelist the domain.

VPN/Corporate Firewall: If the student is on an enterprise network, port 8443 or 2222 might be blocked. Instruct them to disconnect from VPN or try a personal hotspot to isolate the issue.

Issue B: Video Buffering or CDN Authorization Error
SkillSync uses AWS CloudFront signed cookies for DRM video protection.

If a student sees a "403 Forbidden / Expired Token" error on a video, it means their local system clock is out of sync with the NTP server by more than 5 minutes. Instruct them to toggle "Set time automatically" in their OS settings and refresh the browser.
