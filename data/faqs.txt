==================================================
COURSE DISCOVERY & PRE-SALES
==================================================

Q: Do you provide a Full Stack Web Development course?
A: Yes. SkillSync AI offers a 6-month Full Stack Web Development Bootcamp covering HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, APIs, authentication, deployment, and system design.

Q: Do you have AI or Machine Learning courses?
A: Yes. The AI Engineering Program includes Python, machine learning, deep learning, LLM applications, vector databases, LangChain, RAG systems, AI agents, and deployment workflows.

Q: Is there a course specifically for beginners?
A: Yes. Beginner-friendly learning paths are available with no prior coding experience required.

Q: Do you provide placement assistance?
A: Yes. Placement support includes resume reviews, mock interviews, LinkedIn optimization, job referrals, and hiring partner access.

Q: Are the classes live or recorded?
A: Most premium programs include both live mentor-led classes and recorded content for flexible learning.

Q: Do you provide certificates after course completion?
A: Yes. Industry-recognized certificates are issued after successful completion of assessments and projects.

Q: Can working professionals join the courses?
A: Yes. Weekend and evening cohorts are designed specifically for working professionals.

Q: Do you provide internship opportunities?
A: Selected advanced programs include internship and capstone project opportunities with partner companies.

Q: Which programming languages are taught in the AI program?
A: The AI program primarily uses Python along with libraries such as NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, and PyTorch.

Q: Do you teach LangChain and AI agents?
A: Yes. The Generative AI track includes LangChain, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI agents, vector databases, prompt engineering, and LLM application deployment.
